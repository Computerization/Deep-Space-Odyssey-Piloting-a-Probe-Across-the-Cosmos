# Music and free exploration — 0.5

All four music tracks have been replaced with original two-minute chamber arrangements. A shared theme links soft upright piano, bowed strings and occasional horn lines. The four environments differ in register, note density, harmonic colour and orchestration. Total music storage remains about 4 MB. The composer and source credits are in [music-v5.md](../music-v5.md).

Playback uses 7.5-second equal-power transitions, handles rapid repeated chapter changes, retains pause/mute settings and reduces music beneath historical instrument recordings. Interface tones use the score's pitch family at lower volume. The final encoded files measure approximately −23 LUFS, with true peaks below −4.4 dBFS. Browser decoding measured a maximum loop-boundary step below 0.00014 full scale after the encoding taper was adjusted. These are signal and browser measurements, not a claim of perceptual perfection.

Karrik and Sometype Mono replace system-font fallbacks. Fonts and licenses ship locally. Original geometric title lettering and fixed rectangular controls replace arrow-ended, underline-style actions. Readouts retain a distinct monospaced face, and the main scene remains visually dominant.

A straight-line coast predictor shows whether the spacecraft's actual velocity intersects the current observation or arrival region. It gives a miss distance or an arrival estimate at current compression, and explains when braking and re-thrusting are needed. The prediction follows the game's inertial model; steering remains manual. Thirteen observations and four crossings retain their existing progression.

Independent photographs can be taken with P or the Xbox-accessible pause menu. They download and appear in a persistent journal, carrying subject, range, optical field of view and phase. Unresolved planets are excluded from automatic subject identification. Individual field photos can be removed, and they remain separate from the required science observations. If persistent browser storage is unavailable, the downloaded photo is retained and the UI explains the session-only journal fallback.

Ocean glint now uses an isotropic Cox–Munk wave-slope distribution with an assumed 5 m/s wind. This broadens the reflection according to surface roughness; it does not add decorative lighting or measured historical weather. Source imagery and physical angular scales remain in place.

Verification includes unit-level flight predictions, the full thirteen-observation route, actual browser fonts and screenshots, independent-photo persistence and removal, live music output, transition interruption, looping, mute/pause/ducking, synthetic Xbox controls and offline media playback. Physical Xbox hardware and exhibition-speaker listening remain outside the measured checks.
