# Rebuild round 2 — piloting and exploration

Added a close Earth-limb encounter with an off-center scientific sightline. The full route now has 12 observations. Replaced instantaneous targeting with damped steering, fine analogue-stick response and a steady-view exposure requirement. Imaging and signal collection use distinct acquisition durations. Guidance responds to bearing, distance, approach speed and input device, including explicit braking and time-compression cues.

Photographs now capture the real rendered scene, persist in IndexedDB and appear in a downloadable personal photo journal with range, date and lens metadata. Scientific detail is expandable beside the photograph. Added an actual historical-position voyage map with a stated logarithmic distance scale, a clear long-distance crossing display and an optional instruments screen. Preserved manual steering throughout.

The browser test flew the complete updated route using the production movement integrator: 12 observations and four crossings reached the ending with zero browser errors. Review of camera composition, the photo journal, the voyage chart and close-encounter views follows. Round 3 targets audio, scene-specific visual defects, save/recovery robustness and the final exhibition workflow.
